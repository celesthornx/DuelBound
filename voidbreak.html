PROGRESSION

Each level is a chain of 10 sectors ending in a guardian boss.
Beat a level's boss to permanently unlock the next chain.
On the LOADOUT screen: click a weapon to select it — click it again (or press ENTER / START) to launch.
Fall, and your Void Shards carry over to the FORGE — permanent power for future runs.

CHOOSING YOUR PATH

After clearing a room, walk into one of the marked sectors to choose your next room.
Each panel shows the risk and reward — pick what your run needs.
COMBATHigh risk, high reward — waves of enemies, upgrade chance.
COIN RUNNo enemies — a 30-second target range. Every target is pure shards. Combos pay extra.
ELITEVery high risk — a guaranteed upgrade awaits.
TREASUREShard caches, sometimes a weapon cache.
SHRINEA free rare upgrade.
SANCTUMRestore your health.

THREATS · THE ABYSS (LEVEL 4)

THREATS · THE DESCENT (LEVEL 5)

VOID HUNTERKeeps mid-distance, marks you with a pale beam, then fires two precise shots.
GRAVITY LEECHSeeds a swirling gravity field that drags you toward it — escape or kill it to collapse the field.
THE SINGULARITYElite. Gravity, void bursts, collapse zones and a timed shield — punish the shield drop.
VOID PRESSUREBlue zones in the Descent pulse with unstable energy. Watch the warnings — keep moving.
THE DEPTHSWhen its core destabilizes, ATTACK — the exposed core takes heavy damage.

TIPS

Dash grants invulnerability — dodge through bullet rings, hazard zones and gravity fields.
When a guardian shows a gold ring, he is VULNERABLE — unload on him.
Beat THE COLLAPSED ARCHON to unlock the VOID CANNON · beat THE DEPTHS to unlock the GRAVITY ANCHOR upgrade.
THE SENTINEL (Level 6) telegraphs its dash — watch the streaking line and dodge sideways, not backward.
THE ECLIPSE (Level 7) blinks near you and fires on arrival — keep moving so its landing spot isn't yours.
THE ORIGIN (Level 8), like THE DEPTHS, opens when its core destabilizes — that's your window to attack.
Upgrades stack: build crit + explode, or pierce + burn, or pure speed. Every run is different.
